---
title: "Brain health"
description: "Check that a brain is complete, connected and searchable - and fix it when it is not"
---

Every brain has a **Health** page (in the brain's sidebar) that answers one question: is
everything that went in actually findable? It is worth a glance now and then, and it is
the first place to look when a search misses something you know is there.

## The numbers

| Stat | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pages** | Live pages in the brain - the same count the workspace tree shows. |
| **Broken links** | `[[wikilinks]]` pointing at pages that do not exist. Each one is listed below with a link to the page that needs fixing. |
| **Orphans** | Pages nothing links to. Not wrong, but unlinked knowledge is harder to rediscover. Folder index pages are structural and are not counted. |
| **Embed coverage** | How much of the brain semantic search can see (below). |
| **Oversized pages** | Pages over 2,000 words, each listed below. A page should be one topic; these have grown past it (below). |
| **Inbox pending** | Items waiting in the inbox or review queue. |
| **Failed deliveries** | Shown only when a [connected meeting tool](/capture/granola) delivered something in the last week that Cortex could not file; the source row under Settings, Ingest sources says why. |
| **Switched off at the provider** | Shown only when a connected tool reports its webhook disabled; the source row has the link to switch it back on. |
| **Last activity** | The most recent write, human or agent. |

## Embed coverage

Search works two ways at once: keyword match, and semantic match against an embedding of
each page. Embeddings are generated automatically when a page is saved, and an hourly
maintenance pass picks up any that were missed. Coverage is the share of pages (of those
with anything to embed) that semantic search can currently see.

A healthy brain reads **100% and stays there** - green from 90%, amber from 60%, red
below. A brief dip after importing a lot of pages at once is normal; the hourly pass
catches it up. A low number that does not climb on its own is worth fixing: press
**Run dream** to run the maintenance pass now, and if the number still will not move,
contact support.

## Oversized pages

A page is one topic, and the [size rule](/filing/pages-and-structure#the-right-size-for-a-page)
is 300 to 1,500 words. Health counts the pages over 2,000 words and lists them, because a
page that long answers questions badly: search finds it, but the answer is buried. Cortex fixes
them for you. On each pass it takes a few of the listed pages and queues each one for the
**split page** skill, which rewrites the page as a short overview at the same address, so
nothing that linked to it breaks, plus one page per topic underneath. A person, company or
project page in your own brain is [compacted](/filing/pages-and-structure#the-right-size-for-a-page)
instead: rewritten in place with its older history moved to a history page. The split goes through the
brain's filing mode like anything else: a proposal in the [review queue](/filing/review-queue)
on Suggest, applied when confident on Automatic. You can also ask your agent to split a page
now. Folder index pages are never counted.

## Is it still being fed?

The numbers above say whether what went in is findable. They do not say whether anything is
still going in. A brain set up once can quietly stop receiving: the session hook was lost
with a rebuilt laptop, the meeting tool's webhook was switched off, a credential expired, or
items have sat in the inbox for a fortnight. Ask your agent: **"Run the health check on my
brain."** It reads one report and answers, worst first, with the one thing that fixes each:

| Check | What it looks at | Fine when |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | The newest session transcript that arrived through the [hook](/capture/agent-conversations#the-automatic-way-a-session-hook-advanced), and the last use of the credential labelled `claude-code-capture` | A transcript within 3 days |
| **Each source** | A [connected meeting tool](/capture/granola) or webhook: enabled, not switched off at the provider, and delivering | Something within 7 days |
| **Each credential** | Last use and expiry; never the key itself | Used within 30 days and not expired |
| **Inbox** | How long the oldest waiting item has waited | Nothing pending for more than 2 days |
| **Filing** | The brain's filing mode, whether the workspace's monthly limit has paused it, whether Cortex has run recently while items wait, and items it gave up on | Not paused, nothing parked |
| **Search** | Embed coverage | At least 90% |

Those are the defaults. If your brain expects more or less, say so on its instructions
page under a heading `## Expectations`, in plain sentences: "Session transcripts arrive most
days (stale after 2 days)", "Granola transcripts arrive on weekdays through the Granola
source", "No meeting tool is connected to this brain". Your agent reads that section first
and grades against it, so a feed you do not use is never reported as missing. The check
never changes anything: it tells you what to renew, switch on or clear, and you decide.

The same report is there without an agent: `cortex health --brain <slug>` from the
[command line](/connect/command-line#is-it-still-being-fed) prints it and exits with a
code a scheduled job can alert on, and the [API](/reference/api#is-a-brain-still-being-fed)
returns it as JSON.

## The buttons

- **Run dream** runs the hourly maintenance pass immediately: resolves links, fills
  in any missing search data and embeddings, and refreshes the brain's [front page](/filing/pages-and-structure#the-front-page)
  if you have not edited it. Harmless to press whenever - repeated
  presses coalesce into one pass.
- **Repair structure** rebuilds what the brain's template provisioned: its instructions,
  schema and folder index pages. Use it if the Health page warns about stuck page writes
  or template folders are missing. It never touches pages you or your agents wrote.

Both need Admin on the brain.

## Try it

1. Open a brain's Health page and check embed coverage is green.
2. Fix one broken link: open the listed page, correct the `[[wikilink]]`, watch the
   count drop.
3. Press **Run dream** and refresh after a minute to see the counts settle.
4. Ask your agent to run the health check and read which feeds it found.
